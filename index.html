The 51 Shakti Peethas

After Sati ended her life in her father Daksha's yagna, a grief-stricken Shiva carried her body across the world. Vishnu's Sudarshana Chakra divided it, and each place where a part fell became a Shakti Peetha. At each, Shakti is guarded by a Bhairava, a form of Shiva.

Counts vary: 4 Adi Peethas, 18 Maha Peethas (Adi Shankaracharya's stotra) and 51 (Tantra Chudamani / Pithanirnaya). Lists differ between texts and some sites are disputed, so confirm locations before travelling.
